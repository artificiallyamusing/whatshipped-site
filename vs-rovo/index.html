WhatShipped and Rovo Release Notes Drafter

Both draft release notes from Jira, and both stay inside Atlassian. They suit different jobs.

Rovo Release Notes Drafter

Rovo's Release Notes Drafter is a ready-made Rovo agent from Atlassian. At the time of writing (September 2026) it is included with paid Jira and Confluence Cloud plans. You prompt it and it reads up to 20 work items at a time. Atlassian Community posts say the limit can be changed by copying the agent. It groups the work by theme and drafts a Confluence page.

It works on demand, and it does that well. If you release a small number of issues a month and someone is happy to prompt it, Rovo may be all you need. It costs nothing extra on a paid plan.

Please check Atlassian's current documentation, as Rovo changes often.

What WhatShipped does not do

  • Chat, or answer questions about your project.
  • Draft anything other than release notes and digests.
  • Send to Slack, Teams or email. It gives you copy-ready text instead.
  • Publish without a person clicking Publish.

Using both

Many teams can draft ad hoc with Rovo and keep the regular record with WhatShipped. They do not conflict.

Other release-notes apps

The Marketplace has other good release-notes apps, some well established. Depending on the app, you may find:

  • ready-made templates for many formats;
  • direct delivery to email or Slack;
  • public, customer-facing release pages hosted outside Confluence;
  • support for older Atlassian platforms.

WhatShipped does not host public pages or send messages. It keeps all data inside Atlassian, with no external network access, so it can carry the Runs on Atlassian badge. Apps that deliver to outside services usually cannot.

Choose WhatShipped if you want:

  • drafts where every bullet cites its issue and the excluded issues are listed;
  • saved voices per audience;
  • scheduled digests held for review;
  • your changelog as a Confluence page tree;
  • no data leaving Atlassian.

If you need public release pages or direct email delivery today, another app may suit you better. Most offer free trials, so try more than one.
